(Hello)

Hello there, I’m Alhassan, a visual storyteller, filmmaker, and artist. I shoot, create, direct, and edit videos, bringing ideas to life through visuals and creative expression. With over 6 years of experience in the industry, I’ve had the opportunity to work with international brands, including brands operating exclusively across the US and EU. Whether behind the camera or shaping the final frame, I believe in making visuals that speak for themselves.

What I do

(05) Disciplines
01→ Filming Camera work that moves with the story, from lighting the room to finding the shot that says it without words. 02→ Shooting Frames composed with intent, so every product, face and space looks the way it feels. 03→ Editing Rhythm, pacing and cuts that turn raw footage into something people watch to the very end. 04→ Visual director Shaping the whole look, from concept and references to colour and type, so every frame speaks one language. 05→ Post-producer Colour grading, sound design and the final export, the finishing touches that make a campaign feel complete.
